# MyoSense Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-19
**Last updated:** 2026-11-02

This Privacy Policy describes how the MyoSense iOS application (the
"App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use it. The App is
published by Wyatt Flewelling ("we", "us"). By using the App, you agree
to the practices described below.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the
rights described in it, email **wyattflew@yahoo.com**.

**In short:** MyoSense has no accounts and no sign-in. Your recordings
stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is basic,
anonymous app-usage and diagnostic data sent to Google Analytics for
Firebase — never your sensor data, recordings, or session names.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to function. We do **not** sell your
data, and we do **not** use your data for advertising or to track you
across other companies' apps or websites.

### 1.1 No account information

The App does not have accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name,
email address, or password.

### 1.2 Sensor recordings (stored only on your device)

When you record a session, the App saves the following **on your device
only**:

- The raw electromyography (EMG) voltage samples produced by your
  MyoSense sensor hardware, and a computed activation value (TKEO)
  derived from them, as CSV files
- A small session file with the session name you choose, the muscle you
  picked, the start time and duration, and summary activation levels
  ("Resting", "Light", "Strong") and left/right balance

None of this is uploaded to us or to any third party. It leaves your
device only if you export it yourself with the iOS share sheet.

### 1.3 Usage and diagnostic data

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, the App uses
Google Analytics for Firebase, which collects:

- App events (for example, app opens and screen views)
- An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase
- App version, iOS version, device model, and approximate locale

This data is not linked to your name or email (we don't have either).
It does not include sensor readings, recordings, or session names.

### 1.4 Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth to connect to MyoSense sensor hardware near you.
The list of nearby Bluetooth devices is processed only on your device
and is never uploaded.

### 1.5 What we do **not** collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any account details.
- We do not collect your precise location.
- We do not access your photo library, microphone, camera, contacts, or
  calendar.
- We do not use any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

- Operate the App: pair sensors, display real-time EMG data, record
  sessions on your device, and let you export sessions as CSV files.
- Understand, in aggregate, how the App is used and diagnose crashes and
  performance issues so we can fix bugs.

We will **never** use your recordings for advertising, sell them to data
brokers, or share them with insurers, employers, or anyone else. We
cannot see them: they never leave your device unless you export them.

---

## 3. How your data is stored and shared

### 3.1 Storage

Recordings and session files are written to the App's sandboxed storage
on your device and are visible only to you unless you share them
through iOS share sheets. Usage and diagnostic data is stored by Google
Analytics for Firebase on Google Cloud.

### 3.2 Sharing with our processors

We use one third-party service, which acts as our data processor and is
bound by its own privacy terms:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics for Firebase | Usage and diagnostics | App events, app-instance ID, app/iOS version, device model, approximate locale |

Google's privacy practices for Firebase are described at
<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>.

### 3.3 Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other
valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of
users or the public.

### 3.4 No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information as that term is defined in the
California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or any other privacy law.

---

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Recordings and session files:** kept on your device until you delete
  them in the App (Sessions tab, or Settings → Data) or uninstall the
  App.
- **Usage and diagnostic data:** retained by Google Analytics for
  Firebase according to its retention settings for our project, then
  deleted automatically.

---

## 5. Security

- Your recordings never leave your device unless you export them, and
  are protected by iOS data protection.
- The App can ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode when it opens.
- All network traffic between the App and Firebase uses TLS (HTTPS).

No security system is perfect. If we become aware of a breach affecting
your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required
by applicable law.

---

## 6. Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

- **Delete** any session from the Sessions tab, or everything from
  Settings → Data. Uninstalling the App removes all local data.
- **Export** your sessions as CSV files at any time from the session
  summary.
- **Ask about usage data** — email **wyattflew@yahoo.com** with any
  question or request about the usage and diagnostic data described in
  Section 1.3.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your
local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the
rights described in the CCPA, including the right to know what we have
collected and to request deletion.

## 7. Children's privacy

MyoSense is intended for use by adults and by minors under the
supervision of a parent or guardian.
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without
verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided data
to the App without consent, email **wyattflew@yahoo.com** and we will
delete it.

## 8. International users

MyoSense is operated from the United States. If you use the App from
outside the United States, usage and diagnostic data will be transferred
to and processed in the United States and other countries where Google
operates. By using the App you consent to that transfer.
