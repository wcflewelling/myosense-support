# MyoSense Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-19
**Last updated:** 2026-05-19

This Privacy Policy describes how the MyoSense iOS application (the
"App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use it. The App is
published by Wyatt Flewelling ("we", "us"). By using the App, you agree
to the practices described below.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the
rights described in it, email **support@myosense.app**.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to function. We do **not** sell your
data, and we do **not** use your data for advertising or to track you
across other companies' apps or websites.

### 1.1 Account information

When you create or sign into an account we collect:

- Your email address
- Your name (if you provide one)
- A user identifier generated by Firebase Authentication
- The role you select (patient, caregiver, or physician)

This information is required so the App can route patient data to the
correct people and so clinicians can find their patients.

### 1.2 Health and activity data

When you record an EMG session, the App stores:

- The raw electromyography (EMG) voltage samples produced by your
  MyoSense sensor hardware
- A computed activation energy (TKEO) value derived from those samples
- The session start time, duration, and the channel-to-muscle mapping
  you chose
- An activity-level label (e.g. "Resting", "Active") computed by the App
- Optional session notes you enter yourself

This information is linked to your account so you and any clinician you
invite can review it later.

### 1.3 Device and diagnostic data

To keep the App working and to diagnose crashes, we collect:

- Crash reports and performance traces (via Firebase Crashlytics and
  Firebase Performance, when enabled)
- App version, iOS version, device model, and approximate locale

Diagnostic data is not linked to your name or email. It is used only to
fix bugs and improve stability.

### 1.4 Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth to connect to MyoSense sensor hardware near you.
The list of nearby Bluetooth devices is processed only on your device
and is never uploaded.

### 1.5 What we do **not** collect

- We do not collect your precise location.
- We do not access your photo library, microphone, camera, contacts, or
  calendar.
- We do not use any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate the App: pair sensors, display real-time EMG data, record
  sessions, and let you export sessions as CSV files.
- Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- Share session data with the clinicians or caregivers you explicitly
  invite to your workspace.
- Diagnose crashes and performance issues so we can fix bugs.

We will **never** use your health data for advertising, sell it to data
brokers, or share it with insurers, employers, or any party you have
not explicitly invited.

---

## 3. How your data is stored and shared

### 3.1 Storage

Account information and session data are stored in **Google Firebase /
Firestore**, hosted on Google Cloud. Sessions are stored in a database
record keyed to your Firebase user ID. Locally exported CSV files are
written to the App's sandboxed storage on your device and are visible
only to you unless you share them through iOS share sheets.

### 3.2 Sharing with our processors

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each one
acts as our data processor and is bound by its own privacy terms:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication | Sign-in | Email, password hash, user ID |
| Cloud Firestore | Session storage | Account info, session data |
| Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled) | Crash diagnostics | Crash reports, device model |
| Firebase Performance (if enabled) | App stability | Performance metrics |

Google's privacy practices for Firebase are described at
<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>.

### 3.3 Sharing you control

When you invite a clinician or caregiver to your workspace, they gain
read access to your session data through the App's role-based views.
You can revoke their access at any time from the in-app account
screen.

### 3.4 Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other
valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of
users or the public.

### 3.5 No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information as that term is defined in the
California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or any other privacy law.

---

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Account data:** retained while your account is active. Deleted on
  request (see Section 6).
- **Session data:** retained while your account is active or until you
  delete the session in-app.
- **Crash and performance data:** retained for up to 90 days by
  Firebase's default settings, then deleted automatically.

---

## 5. Security

We protect your data using:

- TLS (HTTPS) for all network traffic between the App and Firebase
- Apple's keychain for any locally-stored credentials
- Face ID / Touch ID gating on access to patient session data (when
  enabled on your device)
- Firebase Authentication password hashing and session tokens

No security system is perfect. If we become aware of a breach affecting
your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required
by applicable law.

---

## 6. Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

- **Access** the data we hold about you — email
  **support@myosense.app**.
- **Correct** information by editing your profile in the App.
- **Delete** your account and associated session data by writing to
  **support@myosense.app**. We will complete deletion within 30 days.
- **Export** your sessions as CSV files at any time from the in-app
  recording screen.
- **Withdraw consent** for optional diagnostic collection by disabling
  crash and performance reporting in iOS Settings → MyoSense.
- **Revoke clinician access** from the in-app account screen.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your
local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the
rights described in the CCPA, including the right to know what we have
collected and to request deletion.

---

## 7. Children's privacy

MyoSense is intended for use by adults and by minors under the
supervision of a parent, guardian, or qualified healthcare provider.
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without
verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided data
to the App without consent, email **support@myosense.app** and we will
delete it.

---

## 8. International users

MyoSense is operated from the United States. If you use the App from
outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and
processed in the United States and other countries where our service
providers operate. By using the App you consent to that transfer.

---

## 9. Medical disclaimer

MyoSense is a monitoring aid. It is **not** a diagnostic medical device,
and it does not replace the judgment of a qualified healthcare
professional. Do not use the App to make clinical decisions without
appropriate medical supervision.
